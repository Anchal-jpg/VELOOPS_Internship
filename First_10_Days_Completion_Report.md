## 6. Product Analytics Findings & Readiness Audit

Across the 22 evaluated product areas, data readiness varies significantly:
- **Gamification & Engagement:** Highly ready (88% score). Telemetry captures ad views, daily claims, streak broken events, missions started/completed, and level progression in high volume.
- **Security & CAPTCHA:** Highly ready (90% score). 2,733 events capture start, success, and failure states, revealing a 32.3% failure rate that highlights bot farm pressure.
- **Tasks & Offerwall:** Substantially ready (82% score). 9,846 tasks capture 8 categories and 5 distinct failure modes. The primary gap is the absence of an external partner transaction ID (`partner_transaction_id`).
- **Identity & Funnel:** Partially ready (45% score). Static user profiles exist, but dynamic registration drop-off and login event streams are completely absent.
- **Financial Core & Withdrawals:** Critically unready (8% score). The current dataset pack **does not contain a wallet transaction ledger or a withdrawal table**. In `03_Feature_Events`, wallet interactions are restricted to passive page views (`wallet_viewed`: 2,172 times).

### The 15 Prioritized Analytics Gaps
We identified, quantified, and prioritized 15 critical analytics gaps in [Analytics_Gap_Assessment.xlsx](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/documentation/Analytics_Gap_Assessment.xlsx), led by:
1. **GAP-001 (Critical):** Complete absence of double-entry wallet transaction ledger.
2. **GAP-002 (Critical):** Complete absence of withdrawal payout requests and gateway settlement tables.
3. **GAP-003 (Critical):** Coupled task-reward logging lacking dedicated currency/quantum ledger.
4. **GAP-004 (Critical):** Missing relational referral edge table linking referrers to referees.
5. **GAP-005 (High):** Absence of granular registration and login telemetry funnel stream.

## 7. User & Account Analytics Specification

Detailed in [User_Analytics_Specification.xlsx](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/documentation/User_Analytics_Specification.xlsx) and [User_Account_Analytics_Report.md](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/reports/User_Account_Analytics_Report.md):
- **Identifier Hierarchy:** Strict regex patterns defined for `user_id` (`^VLUSR\d{6}$`), `session_id` (`^SESS\d{7}$`), `event_id` (`^EVT\d{8}$`), and hardware `device_id` (UUID v4).
- **Account State Machine:** 6-stage lifecycle model (`Registered_Unverified` -> `Active_Onboarded` -> `Dormant` -> `Suspended_Fraud` -> `Deactivated` / `Churned_Permanent`).
- **Field Classifications:** 27 verified source profile attributes distinguished from 3 proposed security attributes (`phone_number_verified`, `kyc_tier`, `fraud_risk_score`).
- **Telemetry Funnel:** Proposed event specifications with JSON payloads for `registration_started`, `registration_step_completed`, `email_verified`, `user_login_failed`, and `onboarding_step_viewed`.

## 8. Earning & Reward Analytics Specification

Detailed in [Earning_Reward_Analytics_Specification.xlsx](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/documentation/Earning_Reward_Analytics_Specification.xlsx) and [Reward_Lifecycle_Report.md](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/reports/Reward_Lifecycle_Report.md):
- **Decoupled Architecture:** Eliminating the anti-pattern of logging `reward_status` directly on `05_Task_Activity`. Specifying an immutable `rewards_ledger` with currency type (VE, XP, Gem, Token, Spin), amount, idempotency hash, and escrow timestamps.
- **10-Stage Task Lifecycle:** Mapping Discovery -> Detail View -> Start -> In Progress -> Completion -> Validation -> Escrow Pending -> Credited -> Reversal -> Correction.
- **Empirical Failure Breakdown:**
  - *Validation failed:* 732 (20.50% of failed tasks)
  - *Task unavailable:* 722 (20.22% of failed tasks)
  - *Timeout:* 710 (19.88% of failed tasks)
  - *Incomplete action:* 706 (19.77% of failed tasks)
  - *User exited:* 701 (19.63% of failed tasks)
- **Reward KPIs:** Formal formulas established for Task Completion Rate (57.15%), Reward Reversal Rate ($<1.5\%$), and the Revenue-to-Reward Ratio ($\text{RRR} \ge 2.5\times$).

## 9. Wallet & Withdrawal Analytics Specification

Detailed in [Wallet_Analytics_Specification.xlsx](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/documentation/Wallet_Analytics_Specification.xlsx), [Withdrawal_Analytics_Specification.xlsx](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/documentation/Withdrawal_Analytics_Specification.xlsx), and [Wallet_Withdrawal_Report.md](file:///d:/VELOOPS_DATA_ANALYTICS/VELOOP-Product-Analytics/reports/Wallet_Withdrawal_Report.md):
- **Double-Entry Balance Model:** Proposing an immutable `wallet_transactions` ledger where active balance equals historical credits minus debits, with mandatory `balance_before` and `balance_after` verification.
- **Transaction Taxonomy:** 11 distinct economic transaction types covering faucets, sinks, internal exchange, escrow holds, and chargebacks.
- **5-Stage Withdrawal Pipeline:** `Requested` -> `Processing` (Risk & KYC check) -> `Gateway_Transmitted` -> `Completed` / `Rejected` / `Reversed`.
- **Payout Rails:** Integration specifications for UPI (IMPS Instant), PayPal Payouts, Direct Bank Wire, and Crypto USDT.
- **Anti-Fraud & AML Rules:** Max 2 withdrawals per 24 hours; Tier-0 cashout limit at ₹500; mandatory escrow lock on requested tokens.
